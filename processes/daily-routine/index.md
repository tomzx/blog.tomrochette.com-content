This is the routine I follow on every work day, from catching up in the morning to planning the next day in the evening.
**Most steps are manual, but LLMs assist at fixed points: summarizing yesterday, writing code, and closing the day.**

## Trigger

Every day at the beginning of the (work) day.

## Duration

8h.

## Steps

### Start of day

* Review and correct LLM generated summaries of yesterday's activities
* Catch up on Slack messages (manual)
* [Review PRs](../reviewing-code/index.md) (manual)
* Standup (manual)

### Throughout the day

* [Go to meetings](../meetings/index.md) (some days) (manual)
* [1:1](../one-on-one/index.md) (some days) (manual)
* [Write code](../writing-code/index.md) (mainly LLM assisted)
* Reply to Slack messages (manual)
* [Review PRs](../reviewing-code/index.md) (mainly manual, slightly LLM assisted)

### End of day

* [Continuous learning](../continuous-learning/index.md) (manual)
* [Generate an end of day summary using LLMs](https://github.com/tomzx/dot-claude/blob/main/skills/end-of-day-summary/SKILL.md) (LLM)
* [Daily review](../daily-review/index.md) (manual)
* [Review weekly plan and align](../weekly-review/index.md) (manual)
* [Plan next day](../daily-planning/index.md) (manual)
