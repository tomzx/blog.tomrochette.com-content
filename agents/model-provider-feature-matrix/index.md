Legend: ✓ supported, ✗ not supported, ~ partial or conditional, ? not verified.
Each column links to the provider's canonical pricing page; every cell traces to the guide or to a source cited in its references.

## Reading the matrix

I read this table as a bundle scorecard rather than a price list, because the rows move together in ways the model-level table hides.
**The big three monetize closed frontier weights plus subscriptions, and the four challengers monetize cheap tokens plus open weights, with almost no overlap in which rows they win.**
On the price rows the challengers own the floor, and Moonshot's K3.1 Pro now matches the big-three workhorse rate exactly at $2 / $10, the first challenger flagship to stop undercutting it, while Zhipu's free GLM-4.7-Flash still has no big-three analogue at any price.
On the policy rows the field is closer than list prices suggest, and this is the part I check before switching: DeepSeek's cache discount is the deepest at about one fiftieth on Flash, Alibaba's model tables name a discount and its billing note prices hits at one tenth, and batch is half price at the big three and Qwen, 60 percent of standard at Moonshot, and still unverified at Zhipu.
Long context splits cleanly by flatness: Anthropic (except Haiku 5.5's prompt-length repricing past 100K), DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot K3, and Zhipu keep 1M pricing flat, while OpenAI and Google double past thresholds, which decides whole-repository prompting before any model-quality question.
The weights row is the quiet differentiator: open weights from Alibaba, DeepSeek, Moonshot, and Zhipu mean a private deployment path the closed big three do not offer at all.
The subscription row runs the other way: only OpenAI and Anthropic ship a first-party agent on subscription, and Anthropic's is the one that explicitly does not transfer to third-party harnesses.

## Choosing between providers

- Default workhorse loop: OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google price identically at the tier, so pick by harness fit and let the challenger prices below pressure that default yearly.
- Price-floor BYOK loops: DeepSeek and Zhipu, with Alibaba close behind at flagship quality for $6 out.
- Flat 1M context: Anthropic above the cheap tier, DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot's K3 line (K3 and K3.1 Pro), and Zhipu, the vendors that keep whole-repository reads predictable.
- Coding-specialized spend: Moonshot's k2.7-code (a dated lease, the K2 series retires by end of December 2026) or OpenAI's gpt-5.3-codex, the only two columns with a model built for the job.
- Weights you can host: Alibaba, DeepSeek, Moonshot, or Zhipu, and none of the big three at any price.
- Subscription-first teams: OpenAI or Anthropic, and read the transfer row before assuming the subscription follows your harness.
