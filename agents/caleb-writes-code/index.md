Caleb Writes Code is the YouTube explainer channel of Caleb Eom, covering model releases, AI hardware, and agentic-engineering concepts in short, illustrated videos.
Facts below verified as of 2026-09-02.

**He is the fastest way to stay current in video form, because each release and agentic buzzword becomes a 7 to 13 minute illustrated explainer within days, a release-explainer slot no other member of this category fills.**

## What it is

A YouTube channel started on 2025-03-16 and run by Caleb Eom, whose tagline is "part editorial, part informational on AI".
Coverage falls into four lanes: frontier model releases (Grok, Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen, OpenAI), AI hardware (an OpenAI inference chip, High Bandwidth Flash memory), agentic-engineering concepts ("Graph Engineering explained in 8min", "Why harness is SO expensive", "Agents are slower than LLMs?"), and industry news (OpenAI security incidents, Chinese open-model progress).
Videos run roughly 7 to 13 minutes and use simple illustrations, matching the stated pitch of commentary backed by visuals rather than regurgitated headlines.
He is active on X as calebfoundry, on LinkedIn, and runs a Patreon.

## Status

Active and fast-growing as of 2026-09-02.
The channel reports 107K subscribers and 115 videos, a live counter shows 107,760 subscribers and about 6.7M total views across 115 videos, and uploads run about twice a week, with the latest on 2026-08-27 being an explainer of OpenAI's inference chip.
All of that scale was reached in under 18 months from the 2025-03-16 start.

## Strengths

- Speed and coverage: model releases get an explainer within days, which no other voice in this category matches in video.
- The short, illustrated format lowers the barrier to agentic-engineering vocabulary, the role Karpathy plays conceptually at YouTube speed.
- Cadence is consistent, several uploads a week, so the channel works as a running AI-current-events feed.
- Topic selection tracks what working engineers are asking about this week, from harness costs to agent latency.

## Cautions

- The channel is sponsor-dense: 11 of the last 15 upload descriptions carry a sponsor read or affiliate link, so treat the cuts-through-the-noise pitch as marketing and every sponsored segment as an ad first.
- Explainers compress to fit the format, so verify specifics against primary sources before acting on them.
- I found no Hacker News threads and no substantive Reddit discussions of the channel, so there is no critical community filter on its claims.
- Third-party analytics disagree with themselves: one analytics site's prose claims two years of inactivity while its own FAQ and the channel feed show uploads days ago, so quote its numbers with care.

## Pricing

Free to watch on YouTube.
Revenue is ad and sponsor-funded, with recurring sponsor reads (DevRev, Cline, Micro Center, and others) plus a Patreon.

## Bottom line

**Recommended for engineers who want current-events fluency on models and agentic concepts in minutes, and who will treat each video as a starting pointer rather than a verdict.**
Not for someone who wants tool-tested practice, reference-grade depth, or an ad-free editorial voice.
