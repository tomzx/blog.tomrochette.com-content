Latent Space is the AI engineering publication of record, a Substack newsletter plus a technical podcast hosted by swyx (Shawn Wang) and Alessio Fanelli, and the organizing brand behind the AI Engineer conference series.
Facts below verified as of 2026-09-04.

**Latent Space did not just cover the AI engineer, it named the role and built the conference around it, which makes it the closest thing this domain has to an institutional center of gravity.**

## What it is

A Substack with more than 198,000 subscribers combining a weekly newsletter, a 1-2 hour interview podcast with lab founders, researchers, and tool makers, and a growing event business (AI Engineer Summits across SF and Europe).
The founding essay, [The Rise of the AI Engineer](https://www.latent.space/p/ai-engineer), defined the discipline as distinct from ML research, and the household has extended into AINews daily roundups and an AI-for-science show.
It is also, critically, the place where the tools profiled in this section get their field interviews: Claude Code, coding agents, and synthetic data all feature heavily.

## Status

Active and expanding.
As of 2026-09-02 the announcement month ran "The State of Latent Space" for 2026, adding shows, hosts, and more interview formats; the AI Engineer conference network grew to events in New York, San Francisco, Paris, and Europe.
The podcast regularly lands the people behind the harnesses this section tracks, and swyx's own notes continue to set trends (memory, filesystems, agent-eats-agent).

## Strengths

- The interview depth is unmatched: hour-long technical conversations with people who actually build the tools and models.
- swyx names and popularizes framings (AI Engineer, Software 3.0 conversations, agent ecosystems) before they become consensus.
- The conference and Discord make it a community and a hiring surface, not just a read.
- Editorial breadth spans models, infra, agents, and AI-for-science, so the feed stays useful as the field widens.

## Cautions

- Coverage is lab-and-founder centric, so the down-in-the-trenches practitioner detail is thinner than on a hands-on blog.
- swyx has a direct financial stake in the AI Engineer brand, which colors the "AI Engineer will be the last job" thesis; treat the evangelism as opinion.
- Newsletter and podcast volume can be high for someone who just wants the weekly signal.

## Pricing

Free tier with a paid tier for AINews daily roundups and some deep posts.
The newsletter, podcast, and conference tickets carry their own price points; a meaningful share of flagship interviews is free.

## Compared to

- [Simon Willison](../simon-willison/index.md): the daily hands-on notes versus the interview-and-industry synthesis; follow both for coverage of the same landscape at different zoom.
- [Interconnects](../the-pragmatic-engineer/index.md): Latent Space for the builder community, Interconnects for the researcher's eye on post-training and open models; the difference is audience, not rigor.
- [Steve Yegge](../steve-yegge/index.md): Latent Space interviews the field; Yegge argues with it.

## Bottom line

**Recommended for anyone who wants the industry-level, interview-driven view of AI engineering and the conference network that ties it together.**
Not for people who want only hands-on tool notes and who will not filter past the evangelism.
