# Image prompts for My Feature Planning Method: Iterate in Parallel, Then Pass Forward

Style direction: Flat editorial vector illustration with a drafting-office aesthetic, warm off-white paper background, near-black ink linework, one burnt-orange accent, generous negative space, no gradients or gloss.

## 1. Parallel drafts resolve into one stack

- **Placement**: hero image, directly under the title
- **Purpose**: Sets the article's core visual argument in one glance: chaotic parallel work on the left becoming an ordered sequence on the right.
- **Type**: conceptual scene
- **Alt text**: A draftsman's desk split in two halves, the left strewn with overlapping rough draft sheets and the right showing the same drafts as a single neat vertical stack with one sheet settling into place.
- **Filename**: hero-parallel-then-forward.png
- **Prompt**: A wide flat vector illustration of a draftsman's desk viewed from above, split into two halves by a clean vertical seam. On the left half, a dozen rough draft sheets lie scattered and overlapping at odd angles, translucent, their edges curling, drawn in loose near-black ink. On the right half, the same sheets appear as a single tidy vertical stack aligned to a grid, with one last sheet in burnt orange gently falling into place at the top of the stack. Warm off-white paper background, thin near-black linework, generous empty space along the top edge of the composition, soft even lighting like a drafting table lamp. The mood is calm methodical craft. AVOID: text, lettering, numbers, people, faces, computers, screens, gears, lightbulbs, glossy 3D rendering, gradients, clutter filling the negative space.

## 2. The dependency graph of feature files

- **Placement**: under the heading "files-flow.md turns consistency into a graph", after the first paragraph
- **Purpose**: Makes the central mechanism concrete: documents as nodes with directed content-dependency edges and one highlighted propagation path.
- **Type**: diagram
- **Purpose detail**: Shows why a changed file forces re-verification of everything downstream.
- **Alt text**: A small directed graph of blank document cards connected by arrows, with one arrow chain highlighted in burnt orange showing propagation from a changed card to the cards depending on it.
- **Filename**: files-flow-graph.png
- **Prompt**: A clean flat vector diagram of eight blank document cards drawn as simple rounded rectangles in thin near-black ink, arranged loosely across a warm off-white background and connected by thin directed arrows that form a small dependency graph with no cycles. Most arrows are near-black and quiet. A single chain of two arrows is drawn in confident burnt orange, starting at one card near the top and flowing down to two cards below it, as if that top card has just changed and its dependents are being traced. The cards are blank, no writing on them. Generous white space around the graph, isometric-free straight-on view, precise architectural-drawing linework. AVOID: text, lettering, numbers, labels on cards, people, glowing effects, neon colors, 3D perspective, dense spaghetti wiring.

## 3. Draft sheets betraying their holes

- **Placement**: under the heading "Phase one: iterate in parallel", after the first paragraph
- **Purpose**: Visualizes the question-finding machine: rough parallel drafts whose gaps, not their completions, are the point.
- **Type**: conceptual scene
- **Alt text**: Several translucent rough draft sheets overlapping on a desk, some with ragged irregular holes torn through them, one sheet held up to the light so the holes glow orange.
- **Filename**: parallel-iteration-holes.png
- **Prompt**: A flat vector illustration of five translucent rough draft sheets scattered and overlapping on a warm off-white desk surface, drawn in loose near-black sketch linework. Several sheets have ragged irregular holes torn clean through them. A single hand enters from the lower right holding one sheet lifted up toward the light, so the holes in that sheet glow warm burnt orange against the background, revealing that the gaps were there all along. Soft directional lamp lighting from the upper left, long soft shadows under the scattered sheets, calm investigative mood, plenty of empty paper around the cluster. AVOID: text, lettering, numbers, question marks as shapes, magnifying glasses, lightbulbs, people's faces, glossy rendering, dark moody backgrounds.

## 4. The forward-pass funnel

- **Placement**: under the heading "Phase two: the forward pass", after the first paragraph
- **Purpose**: Draws the colleague's funnel metaphor: the problem statement is the narrow end and each artifact downstream widens it.
- **Type**: diagram
- **Alt text**: A side-view funnel expanding downward, built from stacked blank document layers that grow wider at each step, with the widest bottom layer highlighted in burnt orange.
- **Filename**: forward-pass-funnel.png
- **Prompt**: A clean flat vector diagram of a funnel drawn in thin near-black ink, oriented vertically with the narrow end at the top and widening stepwise toward the bottom, each step formed by a blank document layer drawn as a wider flat rectangle than the one above it. Seven layers descend in strict order, each slightly wider than the last, like a widening staircase of pages seen from the side. The bottom-most and widest layer is filled with a solid burnt-orange wash, the rest outlined on warm off-white paper. A small plain dot marker travels down the center axis of the funnel suggesting a single ordered pass. Straight-on view, architectural precision, large margins on both sides. AVOID: text, lettering, numbers, arrows pointing upward, funnels narrowing downward, 3D rendering, gradients, charts or axes, logos.

## 5. Many threads weave into one line

- **Placement**: at the end of the section "Why two phases instead of one", immediately before the heading "What to Do Next"
- **Purpose**: Anchors the closing argument: the switch between modes is deliberate, many open threads converging into a single forward line.
- **Type**: conceptual scene
- **Alt text**: A handful of tangled parallel ink threads entering from the left and passing through a single small ring, emerging on the right as one straight horizontal burnt-orange line.
- **Filename**: threads-converge.png
- **Prompt**: A minimal flat vector illustration spanning wide: on the left, seven thin near-black ink threads run roughly parallel but uneven, gently tangled and crossing each other. In the center they pass through one small open ring drawn in near-black ink, like a thread guide on a loom. On the right they emerge fused as a single straight horizontal line drawn in solid burnt orange, running cleanly to the edge of the composition. Warm off-white paper background, precise thin linework, enormous negative space above and below, quiet meditative mood with soft even lighting. AVOID: text, lettering, numbers, hands, knots that stay tangled on the right, light rays, glowing effects, dark backgrounds, ornaments.
